# Reproducible artifacts

Simulation results belong here only after a named model version, configuration,
random seed, and validation status are recorded. No result in this repository
may be presented as measured truck performance until it is compared against
independent vehicle or HIL data.

Required metadata for each result:

- model/controller commit,
- plant and component parameter source,
- drive-cycle source,
- random seeds and number of episodes,
- objective weights,
- constraint configuration,
- units and sampling rate,
- validation status and known limitations.

The committed `double_dqn_training_curve.png`,
`double_dqn_training_returns.csv`, and
`double_dqn_performance_dashboard.png`, `double_dqn_representative_trace.csv`,
and `double_dqn_evaluation_summary.json` are reproducible synthetic-environment
outputs generated by `scripts/generate_results.py`. They demonstrate that the
training and evaluation pipeline runs; they are not truck measurements and do
not establish fuel economy, safety, durability, or battery life.
